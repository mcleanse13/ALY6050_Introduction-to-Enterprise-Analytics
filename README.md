ALY6050: Introduction to Enterprise Analytics

Enterprise to Business Analytics – Course Summary

This course provided a comprehensive exploration of business analytics, emphasizing decision-making, optimization, and data-driven problem solving. Throughout six applied projects, I developed practical skills in prescriptive modeling, linear and nonlinear optimization, risk analysis, and scenario evaluation using tools such as Excel and R. The coursework bridged theoretical concepts with real-world applications, enabling me to translate complex business problems into structured models, perform sensitivity analyses, and generate actionable insights for enterprise decision-making.

Across multiple modules, I applied prescriptive analytics and optimization techniques to solve real-world business problems in inventory management, supply chain logistics, and financial decision-making. In Module 4, I designed an inventory management decision model using historical demand data, holding and ordering costs, and simulation in R and Excel to determine optimal order quantities while minimizing total cost under uncertainty. In Module 5, I focused on profit maximization through linear programming, where I optimized the monthly product mix for a new distribution center under budget, space, and sales constraints using Excel Solver and sensitivity analysis. For my final project I modeled a transshipment problem to minimize hazardous waste transportation costs by incorporating intermediate transport nodes and safety constraints, and applied quadratic programming to construct an optimal investment portfolio that balanced expected returns with minimized risk. Sensitivity analyses across these models highlighted trade-offs between cost, risk, and performance, reinforcing the importance of strategic decision-making under uncertainty.

Throughout these projects, I developed strong proficiency in decision methodologies, heuristic optimization, and scenario analysis, translating complex real-world problems into structured analytical models. I applied linear, integer, and quadratic programming techniques, interpreted sensitivity reports and shadow prices, and evaluated how changes in key parameters affect optimal solutions. These experiences strengthened my ability to perform risk analysis and simulation, leverage Excel Solver and R for optimization, and deliver data-driven insights across inventory, supply chain, and financial contexts that supported informed, strategic business decisions.
